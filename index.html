About

EBBLESS plays your playlist or album straight through, without the gaps. Paste a public playlist, album, or track link from Spotify, SoundCloud, or Apple Music and EBBLESS finds and plays each track. Either way, playback threads through so the listening doesn't stop.

Playback

Pre-roll ad handling
The next track is preloaded muted in the background while the current one plays, so any pre-roll ad burns off before it's your turn to hear it. There's no signal that tells EBBLESS an ad is showing — this is a delay heuristic, not a guarantee.
Crossfade between tracks
Overlaps the tail of each track with the start of the next instead of a hard cut. Off by default for a clean, instant gapless handoff.
Crossfade length 4s
How long the overlap lasts.

Library

Clear saved playlists
Removes every playlist saved to your library on this device. Cached matches are cleared too.
