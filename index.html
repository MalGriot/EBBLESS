EBBLESS

Paste a public Spotify playlist link. Tracks are resolved from Spotify's public embed page and matched to YouTube videos (prioritizing official audio/Topic uploads) via a small backend, then played through the YouTube IFrame API with a mute-on-load / auto-unmute timer to blunt pre-roll ads — there's no way to detect or skip an ad through this API, so this is a delay heuristic, not a guarantee.
