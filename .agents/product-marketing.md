## Product Overview
**Product name:** EBBLESS (all caps in prose and UI; "Ebbless" only mid-sentence in running prose)
**Category:** Music player for the playlists you already have. Plays a playlist as one continuous listen.
**One-liner:** EBBLESS turns a playlist into one continuous listen.
**One-sentence pitch:** Bring your music together, keep it moving, and let listening feel like listening again, with no ads from us, no sign-up, and nothing to manage.
**What it does:** Takes a public playlist link (Spotify, plus YouTube, SoundCloud and Apple Music links), finds each song, and plays it straight through as one queue. Layered on a simple surface: different ways to listen (record player, cassette, CD, Cymatics, lyrics, light show, video, cover only), discovery, and search.
**Product type:** Consumer web app (single static page), with Android (sideloaded, not on Google Play) and Mac desktop apps. Android, Mac and PC only; iPhone is out of scope.
**Business model and pricing:** Free. Invite-only beta capped at 50 testers, with a waitlist when full. Pricing TBD.
**Name meaning:** EBBLESS = without ebb. An ebb is a decline or an interruption in the tide. Pronounced EB-less.
**Mission (verbatim, owner):** EBBLESS exists to make listening feel like listening again. We bring your music together, keep it moving, and make discovery feel effortless. No ads. No sign-up. Simple but layered. Music, visuals, nostalgia, curiosity, and the freedom to wander. We believe music should be something you flow through, not something trying to own you.
**Emotional promise:** The music keeps going. You stop managing it.
**Functional promise:** Paste a playlist link. Get continuous playback, matched and queued automatically.

## Target Audience
**Target users:** Everyday listeners, non-technical, who keep playlists and want them to just play. Also people who miss the feel of albums, records and mixtapes.
**Decision-makers:** The listener (B2C, no buyer committee).
**Primary use case:** Put on a playlist and have it run without gaps, re-searching, or babysitting.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Play my playlist from start to finish without fiddling.
- Make a song feel like an event, not a row in a list.
- Find the next song I will love without leaving the flow.
**Use cases:** Background for a day; sitting with one song; singing along with lyrics on screen; watching a song move (Cymatics); sharing a vibe on speakers, TVs and cars.
**Current stage:** Beta (testing began 2026-10-02). Testers are non-technical.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Listening has turned into managing: queues, gaps, interruptions, sign-ups, apps that try to hold your attention.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Big streaming apps put accounts, ads and engagement hooks between you and the song.
- Pasting links and searching song by song is work.
- Plain players treat a song as a row, not a moment.
**What it costs them:** Attention, mood, the flow of a good run of songs.
**Emotional tension:** Wanting to feel the music again, and being pulled out of it.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Other playlist players and aggregators. TBD: name the real ones before using them in copy.
**Secondary:** Streaming apps (Spotify, YouTube, Apple Music, SoundCloud). EBBLESS draws from them and is not affiliated with or a replacement for any of them. Never position against them by name.
**Indirect:** Records, cassettes and CDs (the feeling EBBLESS echoes), and just leaving a playlist on in another app.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- One continuous listen from a playlist link: matched and queued automatically.
- No sign-up, and no ads from EBBLESS.
- Ways of listening, not just a list: record, cassette, CD, Cymatics, lyrics, light show, video, cover only.
- Honest about limits: a pre-roll-ad delay heuristic, not a guarantee.
- Built in the open: a running story of promises made and kept.
**How we do it differently:** Quiet, simple surface with depth underneath. Flow over attention-grabbing.
**Why customers choose us:** The music keeps going, and it feels like listening again.

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| Does it block ads? | No. It cannot detect or skip ads. It mutes briefly in case of a pre-roll ad. A delay heuristic, not a guarantee. |
| Is it a download or conversion tool? | No. It streams. It does not download or convert files. |
| Is it affiliated with Spotify or YouTube? | No. It is not endorsed by or a replacement for either. |
| Why is there no iPhone version? | TBD: owner to state the line. Platform scope is Android, Mac and PC. |

**Anti-persona:** Someone who wants an ad blocker, a downloader, or a Spotify replacement. Anyone wanting offline files.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Gaps, interruptions, accounts, managing the queue.
**Pull:** Paste a link and it plays. Visual, nostalgic ways to listen. Nothing to sign up for.
**Habit:** Existing playlists live in streaming apps.
**Anxiety:** "Is this safe or legal?" Answered by the plain claims: streams, does not download, not affiliated, honest about ads.

## Customer Language
Verbatim customer phrases: TBD. Do not invent. Collect from tester feedback (`EBB-FB-` entries) and add here with the tester's first initial only.
**Product vocabulary (approved):**
- "songs" always, never "tracks" in user-facing copy (owner rule, 2026-10-06)
- "matching" / "re-matching" for the Spotify-to-YouTube step
- "listen", "flow", "keep listening", "music without the ebb"
- "muted briefly in case of a pre-roll ad", "a delay heuristic, not a guarantee"
- A mode name is always followed by what it looks like ("Cymatics is what sound looks like")
**Forbidden terms:** seamless, revolutionary, next-generation, ecosystem, unlock, journey, "experience the future of". Never say EBBLESS blocks, skips or detects ads, or is "ad-free". Never "ad blocker", "downloader", "converter". Never "track" for a song. No dashes in social captions. Never personify the product ("EBBLESS loves music"); speak as "we", the people building it.
**Glossary:**
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Matching | Finding each Spotify song on YouTube so it can play |
| Listening modes | The ways to see a song: LP, cassette, CD, Cymatics, lyrics, light show, video, cover |
| Cymatics | What sound looks like: a live sand-plate pattern behind a small cover |
| Beta | Invite-only test group, 50 spots, waitlist when full |

## Brand Voice
Two registers. The brand guide governs the app UI. VOICE.md governs social, and its section 11 overrides the calm register where they conflict (the owner owns the brand guide).
| Attribute | Means | Doesn't mean | Example line |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plain | Short declarative sentences, say what it does once | Hype, jargon | "Paste a public Spotify playlist link." |
| Honest | State the limit when there is one | Spin, overselling | "A delay heuristic, not a guarantee." |
| Warm (social) | A host talking to someone they want in the room; friendly, enthusiastic, feeling first | Loud, salesy, slangy | "Goosebumps, every single time this chorus comes around." |
| Dry, unbothered | A little understated humor, confident | Sarcasm, jokes at anyone's expense | "Music without the ebb." |
| Candid | Closes the loop: promises tracked, misses told | Vague "soon" | "You asked for it. It is live as of this version." |

**Tone by context:**
- Support: calm, plain, specific.
- Launch: say what shipped, who asked (initials only), what is next.
- Errors: "That doesn't look like a Spotify playlist link." No "Oops".
- Sales: not applicable; invite, never sell.
- Social: feeling first, then the why, then plain words for what you see. Up to 2 exclamation marks. One 🌊 as the only emoji. 3 to 5 hashtags on the last line. No dashes. No greeting opener. One ask per post.
**Terms we own:** "Music without the ebb." "Keep listening." "A continuous player for your playlists."
**Never use (and why):** see Forbidden terms above. Reasons: brand guide, platform terms, and honesty about what EBBLESS can technically do.

## Social Caption Rules (from VOICE.md, restated so drafting skills see them)
- Order: hook, "Why?" line, what you will see and how to get it, story thread, song credit, one ask with 🌊, hashtags.
- Song credit only from the app's now-playing data.
- Claims must be true today. Unfinished work is marked as coming.
- Feeling words only. Dopamine and serotonin are the target feeling, never a claim. No brain, health, mood-benefit or science statements.
- Cross-credit "EBBLESS is made by MAL GRIOT." at most 1 caption in 6.
- Length: under 280 characters for X, 500 for Threads, full text for Instagram.
- Calls to action (existing list): Explore EBBLESS. Try it for yourself. Give your music room to breathe. Experience music differently. Join the beta. Explore the different listening modes. "Discover what's new" only on Ship notes and Fruition posts.
