    The problem

    Listening turned into managing.

    1. Open the app for one song.
    2. Search. Skip. Search again.
    3. Scroll the recommendations.
    4. Forty minutes later, nothing really played.

    Most music apps are built around finding the next thing to play. That's useful. It also keeps pulling you out of the song you're in.

    A different way

    EBBLESS is built for listening.

    Press play and stay. The player is the whole app: the music, something worth looking at, and nothing asking for your attention.

    • ListeningSongs run into each other. Nothing interrupts the queue.
    • WanderingWhen your queue ends, similar songs pick up.
    • DiscoveryPaste a link, search an artist, or describe a mood.
    • ImmersionArt, a record, a cassette, cymatics or lyrics.

    Bring your music

    Paste a playlist. It plays.

    Drop in a link from the places you already listen. EBBLESS finds the songs and queues them.

    • SpotifyPlaylists, albums, songs
    • YouTubePlaylists, videos, Shorts
    • Apple MusicAlbums, playlists, songs
    • SoundCloudTracks and sets

    Keep your subscriptions. EBBLESS never signs in to them. Songs play from YouTube, so a version can differ from the one you know.

    Wander

    Follow your curiosity.

    Type a mood instead of a link, or search an artist. When your queue ends, Discover keeps going in the same lane. There's a switch to turn it off.

    "rainy Sunday, something warm and slow"

    Podcasts work too. Paste a show or an episode.

    Record

    Spin it like vinyl.

    Your album art becomes the label. The platter eases up to speed when you press play and coasts to a stop when you pause.

    Cassette

    Press play on a tape.

    The reels turn and the tape moves from one side to the other as the song goes. Your art is the label.

    Cymatics

    Watch sound take shape.

    Simulated cymatics: sand settling into the patterns of a vibrating plate. Change the pattern, speed and rotation in the app.

    Lyrics

    Follow along.

    Synced lyrics, when a song has them. These lines are samples. Podcasts get captions too.

    Everywhere

    Plays where you are.

    • Lock screen, car, BluetoothPlay, pause and skip from wherever you are.
    • CastChromecast or Google TV from Chrome. AirPlay from an iPhone.
    • FullscreenLet the art, record or cassette fill the screen.

    Try it

    Paste a link. Hear one song.

    A song link plays that song. An album, playlist or artist link plays its first. You get one try.

    On a computer, use the paste box at the top of the player.

    Your one song is playing.

    That was your test. The rest is in the beta.
