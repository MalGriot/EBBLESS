    EBBLESS beta

    Music deserves your attention. Not your attention span.

    EBBLESS is a player for the music you already have, built to be listened to. Press play on the player and hear it.

    The problem

    Listening turned into managing.

    The problem is not just that music became managing. It also got paywalled, and basic features for managing, accessing, and getting a flow going with your music (so you can listen to music for hours) were restricted. It's not a basic function of a music player.

    What we lost

    It stopped being ours.

    We got bombarded with visual and audio ads that interrupt our flow and take us off the track that was connecting us to our music in the first place. We got subscriptions for things that were first given to us for free for years. We lost access to the infinite amount of music on the internet because it got locked behind these platforms. We lost the ability to change our listening experience and mold it for ourselves.

    We got locked into large algorithm systems that care less about what we personally want to listen to and more about what's popular. They are trying to shape our brains into only hearing certain sounds. We also got our data mined and sold, and the companies we are paying are also paying for things we don't necessarily agree on. It's not about us, and these apps are just being rented to us. They're not ours.

    EBBLESS is here to change all that.

    A different way

    EBBLESS is built for listening.

    Press play and stay. The player is the whole app: the music, something worth looking at, and nothing asking for your attention.

    • ListeningSongs run into each other. Nothing interrupts the queue.
    • WanderingWhen your queue ends, similar songs pick up.
    • DiscoveryPaste a link, search an artist, or describe a mood.
    • ImmersionArt, a record, a cassette, cymatics or lyrics.

    Bring your music

    Paste a playlist. It plays.

    Drop in a link from the places you already listen. EBBLESS finds the songs and queues them.

    • SpotifyPlaylists, albums, songs
    • YouTubePlaylists, videos, Shorts
    • Apple MusicAlbums, playlists, songs
    • SoundCloudTracks and sets

    Keep your subscriptions. EBBLESS never signs in to them. Songs play from YouTube, so a version can differ from the one you know.

    Search anything

    One box. Type or paste almost anything.

    EBBLESS works out what you gave it. Tap an example to watch it go into the box on the player.

    Artists and shows

    Link a whole artist. Get everything they've made.

    Paste an artist page, or a podcast show, from any of your sources. EBBLESS brings in their catalog or every episode, in one place, ready to play.

    • Artist pagesSpotify, Apple Music, YouTube, SoundCloud
    • Podcast showsA show, an episode or an RSS feed

    Click an artist's name in the player to open their page. Try it on the player now.

    Wander

    Follow your curiosity.

    Type a mood instead of a link, or search an artist. When your queue ends, Discover keeps going in the same lane. There's a switch to turn it off.

    "rainy Sunday, something warm and slow"

    Podcasts work too. Paste a show or an episode.

    Record

    Spin it like vinyl.

    Your album art becomes the label. The platter eases up to speed when you press play and coasts to a stop when you pause.

    Cassette

    Press play on a tape.

    The reels turn and the tape moves from one side to the other as the song goes. Your art is the label.

    Cymatics

    Watch sound take shape.

    Simulated cymatics: sand settling into the patterns of a vibrating plate. Change the pattern, speed and rotation in the app.

    Lyrics

    Follow along.

    Synced lyrics, when a song has them. These lines are samples. Podcasts get captions too.

    The game

    There's a game hiding in the record.

    Go fullscreen with the record showing and double-tap it to start a run. Tap as the album art comes back upright. Every hit speeds the platter up. Miss, and the run ends. Your best score is kept.

    Color

    The whole app changes color with you.

    The color of EBBLESS moves with the time of day, and takes on the color of the song that's playing. Watch it change from one song to the next.

    Everywhere

    Plays where you are.

    • Lock screen, car, BluetoothPlay, pause and skip from wherever you are.
    • CastChromecast or Google TV from Chrome. AirPlay from an iPhone.
    • FullscreenLet the art, record or cassette fill the screen.
