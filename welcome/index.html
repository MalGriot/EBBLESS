    A note from the maker

    Why are we paying more to get less?

    Somewhere along the way, music became a chore. Subscriptions for the basics. Ads in the middle of the good part. Playlists picked for you, not by you.

    I made EBBLESS to give listening back to you.

    Peace and love,
    Mal
    EBBLESS Dev

    Bring your music

    Paste a link. It plays.

    From the places you already listen. Playlists, albums, songs, whole artist pages and podcast shows.

    • Spotify
    • Apple Music
    • YouTube
    • SoundCloud
    • Podcasts

    Keep your subscriptions. EBBLESS never signs in to them. Songs play through YouTube, so a version can differ from the one you know.

    Search anything

    One box. Type or paste almost anything.

    Tap an example and watch it land in the search bar.

    Artists and shows

    Link an artist. Get everything they've made.

    Paste an artist page or a podcast show and EBBLESS brings in the whole catalog, or every episode, ready to play.

    Click an artist's name in the player to see who they are. Try it on the player now.

    Discover

    Follow your curiosity.

    Type a mood instead of a link. When your queue ends, Discover keeps going in the same lane. There's a switch to turn it off.

    "rainy Sunday, something warm and slow"

    Record

    Spin it like vinyl.

    Your album art becomes the label. The platter eases up to speed when you press play and coasts to a stop when you pause.

    Cassette

    Press play on a tape.

    The reels turn and the tape moves from one side to the other as the song goes. Your art is the label.

    Cymatics

    Watch sound take shape.

    Sand settling into the patterns of a vibrating plate, turning slowly. Change the pattern, speed and rotation in the app.

    Lyrics

    Follow along.

    Synced lyrics, when a song has them. These lines are samples. Podcasts get captions too.

    The game

    The record is also a game.

    Remember the dinosaur in Chrome's offline page? One button, perfect timing. This is that, with a record. Watch it play itself.

    1. Go fullscreen with the record showing.
    2. Double-tap the record to start a run.
    3. Tap each time the album art comes back upright. Every hit speeds it up.
    4. Miss one and the run ends. Your best is kept.

    Color

    The whole app changes color with you.

    Its color moves with the time of day, and takes on the color of the song that's playing. Watch the player.
