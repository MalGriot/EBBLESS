Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-10-18. EBBLESS is in private beta, so this will change. Written from what the app's code actually does, not from a template.

The short version.
  • Your library, playlists and settings live on your device. If you sign in with Google, a copy is stored on our server so it follows you between devices.
  • EBBLESS watches what you play, finish, skip and like to shape Discover and your picks. You can turn that off and erase it in Settings, under Privacy & data.
  • There is no advertising and no third-party tracking or analytics code in the app. Beta testers' usage is counted for us (details below).
  • Music comes from other services (YouTube and others). They get some information when you use them. See "Other services".

1. Who runs EBBLESS

EBBLESS is run by MAL GRIOT. To reach us about privacy, use Settings → Send feedback in the app, or open an issue at github.com/MalGriot/EBBLESS.

Needs a decision before public release: the legal name of the operator, a postal address or dedicated privacy email, and the country the operator is legally based in. None of these are stated here because they are not in the code.

2. What is stored on your device

EBBLESS keeps this in your browser or app storage (localStorage, plus a cache for the app files). It stays there until you clear it, clear site data, or uninstall.

  • Your library: saved playlists and their tracks, pins, Liked Songs, play counts, last-played times, resume position, queue history.
  • Settings: blend, volume, visuals, accent, sort and view choices, and the Personalized listening switch.
  • Play history: a list of up to 500 recent plays (title, artist, art, time), shown in the app.
  • Learned preferences (see section 5): taste data for up to 1,500 songs, a genre-link table, thumbs-downs, a cache of genre tags, a list of recent plays (up to 400) that seeds your picks.
  • Listening recap log: per song, playlist, podcast and feature, how long and how often you listened, and which days. Used for the tester recap.
  • Technical: a random device ID (used for handoff between devices), your beta tester token, sign-in session, a local crash log, a log of "wrong song" reports, and (if you use captions) an on-device speech model and caption cache.
  • If you connect Spotify or Last.fm to import: a Spotify access token or a Last.fm username is kept on your device.

3. Accounts and sign-in

Google sign-in is optional. When you use it, our server verifies your Google token and gets your Google account ID and email address. It creates an EBBLESS login session (stored on your device; the server keeps only a hash of it, for up to 90 days of inactivity). Your email is shown in Settings and stored with your cloud profile.

The private beta uses an invite. When you sign up we collect your name or nickname, email, and your answers to the sign-up questions (such as a playlist link and music taste), plus your browser's user-agent string. These go to a Google Sheet that the operator uses to approve testers and send invites. Testers are identified by a tester token.

Feedback and bug reports you send include what you type and technical details (device, operating system, browser, screen size, and for wrong-song reports, the song and the match that played). They go to the same Sheet or to our server's report storage.

5. Listening and personalization

EBBLESS learns from what you do: songs you play, finish, skip early, like, or thumbs-down. It uses this to rank Discover and CuRRentSSsss picks and to avoid repeating songs you just heard. Specifically it builds, on your device:

  • per-song counts (days played, finishes, early skips),
  • a table of which genre tags go together for you,
  • your thumbs-downs (also synced if you are signed in),
  • a list of recent plays used to seed new picks.

To look up genre tags, the title and artist of songs you play are sent to our server, which asks Last.fm and similar sources.

The shared tag pool. When you finish, like or skip a song, EBBLESS sends our server a short list of that song's genre tags (up to 8) and a small positive or negative weight. The server adds this to anonymous counters of which tags pair well. No account ID, device ID or song name is sent with it, and none is stored by us. The stored counters carry no identifier, so we cannot find or delete your individual contribution later. EBBLESS also reads the pool to give new listeners a starting point.

Your control: Personalized listening

In Settings → Privacy & data, the "Personalized listening" switch is on by default. When you turn it off:

  • plays, finishes, skips and likes stop being recorded as taste data, genre links, or the recent-plays list,
  • nothing is sent to the shared tag pool and the pool is not read,
  • what was already learned is kept on your device but not used. Turn the switch back on and it is used again,
  • explicit actions still work: Liked Songs, playlists, and your thumbs-downs (a thumbs-down is something you told us directly).

The setting is saved on your device and, if you sign in, follows your account to your other devices.

Turning it off does not stop the play history list you can see in the app, the listening recap log, or per-playlist play counts. Those are your own records, not taste learning. See section 8 for how to clear them.

Your control: Reset listening profile

The same section has "Reset listening profile". It erases the taste data, genre links, thumbs-downs, genre tag cache and recent-plays seed list on that device. It does not delete playlists, Liked Songs or the library. Thumbs-downs are removed from your cloud profile at the next sync. Other devices keep their own taste data until you reset them too.

6. Analytics and tracking

  • EBBLESS contains no advertising, no third-party analytics SDK, and no tracking pixels.
  • Beta usage counts. If you are a beta tester (you have a tester token) and use the real site (not localhost), the app sends our server counts only: sessions, listening time, songs and playlists played, playlists added, and which features you used. Not song names, playlist names, links, or device details. These go into the operator's Sheet against your tester number. Sent every 10 minutes while open and when you leave the tab. There is currently no switch for this in Settings; a developer console command exists, which is not good enough, and is listed as an open item for the operator.
  • The 15-minute sample runs in a throwaway store and is blocked from sending account, profile or usage data.
  • Our server (on Cloudflare) receives your IP address with each request, as every web server does, and Cloudflare may keep standard request logs. We have not verified Cloudflare's retention for this deployment.

8. Keeping, deleting and your rights

  • On your device: kept until you remove it. Settings → Library & data → "Clear saved playlists" removes the library. Clearing your browser's site data, or uninstalling the app, removes everything stored on that device, including play history and the recap log.
  • Cloud profile: kept until you delete it. When signed in, Settings → Account → Delete cloud data erases your stored profile from our server and signs you out. Your local library stays. Other devices that are still signed in could upload their copy again if they sync, so delete after signing out there, or delete again.
  • Beta records (sign-up details, tester analytics, feedback): kept in the operator's Sheet until the operator removes them. There is no self-service deletion. Ask through Send feedback and the operator will remove them by hand.
  • Login sessions on other devices expire on their own after up to 90 days.
  • Tag pool contributions are anonymous and cannot be removed individually.
  • Rights. Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your data, to object to processing, and to complain to a regulator. Ask through Send feedback. There is no data export button yet.

There is no automatic retention schedule beyond the 90-day session expiry and the 180-day removal memory.
