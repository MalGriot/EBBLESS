1. What EBBLESS is

EBBLESS is a music player. You paste a link to a playlist, album or song from a service like Spotify, SoundCloud or Apple Music, and EBBLESS finds each song and plays it, mostly through YouTube. It is run by MAL GRIOT.

EBBLESS does not host or own the music. Songs are played from other services (mainly YouTube) and information comes from others such as Spotify, Apple Music, SoundCloud, Last.fm and more. Those services are independent of EBBLESS and have their own terms and privacy policies. EBBLESS is not endorsed by or affiliated with any of them. By playing music through EBBLESS you are also using their services, and you agree to follow their terms, including YouTube's Terms of Service. We can't promise a song will be available, correct, or the version you wanted. Songs sometimes match the wrong video; you can report it from the player.
