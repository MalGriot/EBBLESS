01 — Brand essence

What EBBLESS is, in one breath.

EBBLESS takes a Spotify playlist, finds each track on YouTube, and plays it back as one continuous queue. It's built to keep the listening going — not to fight the platforms it draws from.

The name

EBBLESS = without ebb. Not a made-up tech word — a real one, subtracted from. It names the specific thing the product removes: the interruption, the dead air, the retreat back to a search bar.

Mission

Keep the listening going. That's the whole job. Everything else — matching, caching, the mute timer — is in service of that one sentence.

Personality

Quiet, precise, a little dry, unbothered. It explains itself once, plainly, and moves on. It does not perform enthusiasm.

Positioning

Not an ad blocker. Not a Spotify alternative. A continuous player that sits on top of playlists you already have, built by one person who wanted the music to stop stopping.

Emotional promise

The music keeps going. You stop managing it.

Functional promise

Paste a playlist link. Get continuous playback, matched and queued automatically.

02 — Logo system

The wordmark is the logo.

No music note, no headphone icon, no waveform badge sitting next to the name. The concept lives in the letterforms: one unbroken line running the full width of the word — because nothing in the mark is allowed to visually "ebb."

Favicon, contained16-64px

Minimum size & clear space

Wordmark: never below 14px cap-height. Below that, switch to the compact mark. Clear space on all sides equals the wordmark's own cap-height — don't let UI chrome or edges crowd it.

Concept, not decoration

The rule beneath the wordmark is one continuous stroke — it never breaks under the gap between the two Bs, never dashes, never lifts. That's the whole idea: an unbroken line under an unbroken word.

05 — Visual language

Continuity, not wave clip‑art.

The idea is a line that doesn't break — expressed sometimes literally (the wordmark's rule, a seek bar) and mostly abstractly (dark, quiet fields; album art left alone to be the visual hero). It is not a brand made of ocean-wave graphics.

Negative space is a feature

Dark, quiet fields around small warm accents. Don't fill space to look "designed" — EBBLESS should feel like it has room to breathe between songs the way a good DJ set does.

Album art is the hero

When something is playing, the track's own artwork carries the visual interest. Brand chrome recedes — no overlays, no colored filters on top of artwork.

One motif, used once

The continuous stroke appears at most once per screen — a seek bar, a loading line, the wordmark rule. Never as a repeating background pattern.

Corners & surfaces

6-8px radius throughout, circular only for transport controls. One radius scale, no mixing. No glassmorphism beyond the player bar's existing blur.

06 — Motion

Always moving forward, never bouncing back.

EBBLESS is fundamentally about continuity, so motion should feel continuous — not flashy. Nothing overshoots, springs, or calls attention to itself.

A single continuous line filling left-to-right — never a spinner, never a shimmer grid.

Timing

150-250ms for hover/active/panel-open. 400-600ms for track or view changes. ease-out for things entering, ease-in-out for things moving in place.

Never

Bounce or elastic/spring easing. Particle effects. Waveform visualizers as chrome. Staggered bounce-ins. Animated gradients. Pulsing "AI is thinking" dots.

07 — Voice & language

Say it once, plainly, and stop.

The existing README already nails the tone — "there's no way to detect or skip an ad through this API, so this is a delay heuristic, not a guarantee." Exact about limits, no spin. That's the reference for everything EBBLESS writes.

Avoid

"Seamless," "revolutionary," "next-generation," "ecosystem," exclamation points, personifying the product, technical explanations unless necessary.

Legal / product-language constraint

Never say EBBLESS "blocks," "skips," or "detects" ads — the YouTube API gives no such signal. Never claim affiliation with Spotify or YouTube. It streams; it doesn't download or convert.

10 — Do / Don't

Keep it short.

  • ✓Keep one continuous accent color
  • ✓Let silence and dark space do the work
  • ✓Describe the mute/unmute heuristic honestly
  • ✓Keep the wordmark's underline unbroken
  • ✓Crossfade between tracks
  • ✕Call it an ad blocker
  • ✕Add a second accent color
  • ✕Add a mascot or symbol "for branding's sake"
  • ✕Animate with bounce or elastic easing
  • ✕Use Spotify green or YouTube red
