    What testers are saying

    Questions

    What is EBBLESS?

    A music player for the music you already have. Bring a playlist from Spotify, YouTube, Apple Music or SoundCloud, and EBBLESS plays it straight through, keeps it moving, and gives you something to look at while it plays.

    How do I get access?

    Tap Join the beta and fill in the short form. We read every request. If you're approved, we email you a personal link that opens EBBLESS.

    What happens after I join?

    You'll see Request received here and get an email saying we've got it. Then we review your request. There's nothing else you need to do.

    How long does approval take?

    Every request is reviewed by hand, so it isn't instant and there's no fixed time. If you're approved, you'll hear from us by email.

    What happens after I'm approved?

    We email you your personal access link right away. Tap it and EBBLESS opens. About a day later, a second email brings your feedback form, and your 7 days start.

    Where will I receive my access link?

    At the email you signed up with. It comes from EBBLESS BETA with a subject that starts with You're in. If you can't find it, check spam or promotions.

    What is the 7-day test period?

    Your 7 days to actually use EBBLESS and send your feedback. They start with the feedback email, about a day after you're approved.

    What happens after 7 days?

    This round of testing wraps up. The next time you open EBBLESS, you get one last song, then a recap of your week: what you played most, your favorite artists, albums and playlists, and the features you used. The feedback form is right there if you haven't sent yours yet. Your listening history stays on your device (and in your account, if you sign in with Google), so if you join a future beta, it's all waiting for you.

    I'm already a tester. How do I get into EBBLESS?

    Open the You're in email and tap your personal link. That's it. If the link doesn't open the app, open EBBLESS and paste the link where it asks. If you've used EBBLESS on another device and signed in with Google there, you can sign in with the same Google account.

    What devices and browsers work?

    EBBLESS runs in your web browser on phones, tablets and computers. Nothing to download from an app store. On a phone it asks you to add it to your home screen first, and shows you how.

    How do I give feedback?

    Use the feedback form in your email, or tap Send feedback inside the app (in Settings). Something broken? Tell us there too. Honest beats nice.
