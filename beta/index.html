GOT IT.

What it is

EBBLESS plays your playlist straight through, without the gaps.

Bring the music you already have, from wherever you already listen. EBBLESS finds every track, keeps it moving, and gives you something to look at while it plays. Simple on top, more underneath when you go looking.

What you can do

  • Bring any playlistPaste a Spotify, YouTube, Apple Music or SoundCloud link. EBBLESS matches the tracks and queues them up.
  • Or just say the vibeType a mood or a moment instead of a link and get a playlist back for it.
  • It keeps goingTracks flow into each other. When the playlist runs out, Discover keeps going with music in the same lane.
  • Something to look atSwitch between LP, cassette, cymatics, lyrics and the music video. Go fullscreen.
  • Plays where you areLock screen, car and Bluetooth controls. Cast to Chromecast or Google TV, or AirPlay from an iPhone.
  • Podcasts tooPaste a podcast link and it plays the show's own episodes.

Each YouTube track starts muted for a moment in case of a pre-roll ad. It's a delay trick, not a guarantee.

How it works

  1. Hit Join the beta and fill in the short form.
  2. I go through every signup by hand. If you're in, your tester link comes to your email.

  5. Load a playlist, try the visual modes, and tell me what works and what doesn't. Feedback and bug forms are one tap away.
