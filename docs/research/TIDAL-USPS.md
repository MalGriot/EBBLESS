# Tidal: what makes it different, and what fits EBBLESS

Covers Geethub #542. Research only, no app code changed. Date: 2026-10-18.

Method: public web pages and press coverage (Wikipedia snapshot, 2026 reviews,
trade coverage of Tidal's 2021 and 2024 plan changes). tidal.com and its
support site blocked our fetch, so plan names and prices below come from
third parties and disagree with each other. Re-check on tidal.com before
quoting any number. Anything we could not confirm is marked.

Mission lens, from the README: "make listening feel like listening again,"
no ads, no sign-up, simple but layered, discovery that feels effortless, music
you flow through and that does not try to own you.

## What Tidal is known for

1. **Sound quality.** Lossless (CD quality) on every paid plan, high-resolution
   up to 24-bit/192 kHz, and Dolby Atmos spatial audio. Tidal dropped MQA
   (a compressed hi-res format) in 2024 and Sony 360 Reality Audio the same
   year.
2. **Paying artists.** Tidal launched in 2014 as an artist-owned brand and
   still markets itself on fairer pay. In 2021 it announced direct payments
   (a slice of a fan's fee going to the artist they listen to most) and
   "fan-centered" royalties, where your money goes to what you actually play
   instead of a shared pool. After the 2024 plan merge we could not confirm
   whether those payments survived, so do not claim it.
3. **Credits.** Tap a song and see who wrote, produced and played on it, and
   click through to their other work (since 2019). The labels and artists
   supply this data.
4. **One plan, no tricks.** Since April 2024 the old HiFi and HiFi Plus tiers
   became one plan. Reported price is about $10.99 to $11.99 a month (sources
   disagree; one says a rise to $11.99 starts August 2026). No free tier since
   2024, only a trial.
5. **DJ Extension.** A paid add-on that lets DJ software and hardware use
   Tidal tracks, including splitting a song into vocals, drums and so on
   (about $9 a month, one source).
6. **Live and video.** Concert livestreams, music videos, a yearly "My
   Rewind" recap.
7. **Fewer AI tracks in the pay pool.** From July 2026 fully AI-made tracks
   are labelled and earn no royalties (Wikipedia). Not confirmed elsewhere.
8. **Exclusives.** Early on it won big artist-owner exclusives such as
   Beyonce and Kanye West albums. Less of a pull today.
9. **Other things seen in the past, not confirmed now:** Tidal Connect (send
   playback to speakers), editorial curation and artist-written "Rising"
   features. We could not verify current details.

## Which fit EBBLESS

EBBLESS plays YouTube and SoundCloud audio. It cannot offer Tidal's audio
quality, and should not claim to. The lessons are about presentation and
values, not sound.

| Tidal USP | Fits mission? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Lossless / hi-res / Atmos | No | EBBLESS streams YouTube/SoundCloud audio; quality is capped by the source. Do not promise it. |
| Credits (who made this song) | Yes, strongly | Discovery and curiosity. A tap from a song to its writer, producer or sample source is "freedom to wander." Builds on the artist and album pages already in the app. |
| Paying artists fairly | Partly | Aligned with the spirit, but EBBLESS does not collect money, so it cannot pay anyone. What it can do is send listeners to the artist: a clear "support this artist" link (their page, Bandcamp, merch) that never feels like an ad. |
| Fan-centered royalties | No | Needs payments and accounts. |
| Livestreams, exclusives | No | Content deals, not product features, and exclusives fight the "no walls" idea. |
| DJ Extension / stems | Maybe later | Matches "layered" and the creative side of the audience, but it is a big, license-heavy build. |
| "My Rewind" yearly recap | Yes | EBBLESS already tracks plays locally (README: playLog). A year-in-music card the listener can screenshot fits nostalgia and costs no sign-up. |
| One simple plan | Yes (already true) | EBBLESS is free with no tiers. Keep it that way. |
| Quality labels on tracks | No | Misleading for a YouTube-based player. |

## Recommendations, ranked

Effort: **small** under half a day, **medium** one to two days, **large**
several days.

1. **Song credits on the song page.** Show writer, producer and featured
   artists when known, each tappable to see more from that person. Medium.
   The strongest fit: pure discovery, no account, and the song/artist pages
   already exist. Data would have to come from a free open source (for example
   MusicBrainz); check coverage and licence before committing.
2. **A yearly (or any-time) "your listening" recap card.** Top songs, top
   artists, minutes listened, shareable as an image. Medium. Uses data the app
   already keeps on the device and fits nostalgia and curiosity. Keep it
   opt-in and local.
3. **A quiet "support this artist" link on artist pages.** Small. Their own
   site or Bandcamp, only where we know the real link. Echoes Tidal's
   artist-first stance without EBBLESS handling money. No popups, no begging.
4. **Say what we are on our welcome/about copy, not Tidal's pitch.** Small.
   One honest line: "plays the best available audio from YouTube and
   SoundCloud." Avoids ever implying hi-res and keeps trust.
5. **Park:** stem splitting for DJs, livestreams, anything with a paywall or
   exclusive. Large and a poor fit right now.
6. **Revisit later:** a Tidal link as a fourth playlist source (read the
   title/artist like Spotify and Apple links). Medium, only if testers ask,
   since Tidal is a small share of listeners compared with Spotify.
